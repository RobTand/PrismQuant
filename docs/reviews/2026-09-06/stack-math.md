## Math conclusions

* HT point estimate is correct at measured rungs under the actual draw, conditional on finite Fisher sizes and truthful positive inclusion probabilities. Each expert contributes y_e=sum_roles(h_e/R)*mse_role. Dividing estimated sum by h_stack makes allocator_solver.predicted_dloss's 0.5*h_stack product reproduce the existing per-expert currency. This validates consistency with that contract, not a new claim that the proxy equals served KL.
* Production take-all iteration solves pi=min(1,c*h) and random permutation + uniform systematic start gives the recorded first-order pi. Zero h is correctly omitted; all-positive census has zero sampling variance; one random draw correctly refuses. New test calls the production sampler and estimator 4,000 times each for n=8 and n=16 on the real LFM down-projection fixture; fixed sample size, empirical inclusion frequencies and unbiased HT all pass. It also uses the same actual sample for both rungs and their paired difference. The older tests (`test_tessera_stack_sample_cost.py:144-164,449-452`) used independent Bernoulli draws and did not establish fixed-size-design behavior.
* Gate/up projections must share expert IDs because StackExpertSample sums them before HT; the original planner (`dispatch_tessera_campaign.py:354-360`) used distinct role seeds. Sampling once per packed parameter, preserving the full frame and reusing the draw for all rungs fixes the design mismatch. Down projection may use its own design because its Fisher vector differs. Cross-rung marginal uncertainty needs applying the same estimator to per-expert differences; sqrt(SE_1^2+SE_2^2) would discard covariance. Current allocation does not consume this SE, so no new uncertainty policy was introduced.
* Reported HR variance (`tessera_campaign.py:744-758,784-788`) is approximate. Exact counterexample: equal h over N experts, fixed m<N draw. Randomized permutation makes this SRSWOR. Put z_i=y_i/pi_i, pi=m/N. Code v=m/(m-1)*(1-(m-1)/N)*sum_s(z_i-zbar)^2; exact unbiased SRS variance estimator has factor 1-m/N. Thus E[v]/Var(T_hat)=(N-m+1)/(N-m), reaching 2 when m=N-1. No assertion of exactness or universal conservatism is justified. Randomization also means fixed-order zero pairwise probabilities do not establish zeros for the actual design: equal-weight pi_ij=m(m-1)/(N(N-1)) is strictly positive for m>=2.
* `draw_stack_sample` docstring says uniform sampling is biased (-6..-28%). Uniform sampling with its correct HT weights is unbiased; that statement confuses sampling design and estimator. No fallback/default change is proposed.
* Interpolated rows apply a nonlinear log interpolation to measured HT anchors and should not inherit a claim of design-unbiasedness. Existing payload marks them interpolated, so no additional defect is asserted.

Primary-source support: Matei's original research thesis, variance approximation section 4.3, equations 4.13 and variance estimators section 4.4: https://libra.unine.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/93669154-3cd3-4ce4-8a12-48765e2e27f2/content . Its Hartley-Rao discussion explicitly describes an approximation. Chen, Gu and Wu (2008) discuss randomized systematic PPS joint inclusion probabilities and exact versus approximate computation: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/12-001-x/2008001/article/10613-eng.pdf . The exact equal-weight counterexample above is independently derived and needs no simulation.
